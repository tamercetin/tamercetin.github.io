Machine Learned Causal Estimates of Dietary Intake on Blood Pressure

Authors: Tamer Cetin and co‑authors

This empirical working paper applies double machine learning to estimate the causal effect of dietary intake on blood pressure in a large observational dataset. By using flexible machine learning methods to control for confounders and employing cross‑fitting, the analysis produces reliable estimates of how specific nutrient intake affects systolic and diastolic blood pressure. The paper demonstrates how modern causal inference tools can uncover relationships in public health data while mitigating bias.
