Adaptive Double Machine Learning via Riesz‑Risk Cross‑Validation

Authors: Tamer Cetin and co‑authors

This working paper introduces an adaptive double machine learning (DML) estimator that leverages Riesz‑risk cross‑validation to select tuning parameters for first‑stage machine learning models. By optimizing the Riesz representer risk, the method improves the finite‑sample performance of the estimator while preserving the orthogonality and robustness properties of traditional DML. The paper highlights the importance of cross‑fitting, cross‑validation, and appropriate regularization when estimating causal parameters in high‑dimensional settings.
