Illustrative output

What a decision memo looks like

Example: pricing and demand scenario for a large multi-series business.

Recommended action Increase price by 3% in Segment A while holding Segment B constant; expected revenue impact remains positive under the current demand regime.

Forecast view: Demand softening is concentrated after a structural break in Q4, so post-break models dominate the pre-break baseline.

Causal view: Observational elasticity estimates suggest a moderate response, with uncertainty bands narrow enough for a controlled rollout.

Operational view: Launch as a monitored pilot, compare against holdout units, and update the policy recommendation weekly.

What the platform does

Most enterprise tools either predict or report. We are building a platform that combines advanced forecasting, modern machine learning, and causal inference so teams can ask not only what is likely to happen, but what will happen if they act.

Forecasting for unstable business environments

  • Many-series and hierarchical forecasting
  • Probabilistic intervals and scenario-conditioned forecasts
  • Structural-break and regime-shift awareness
  • Linked-series modeling for price, demand, supply, and revenue systems

Prediction systems built for large data

  • Machine learning for large-scale business data
  • Cross-series feature learning and exogenous driver integration
  • Champion-challenger evaluation and rolling backtests
  • Reliable model switching when business dynamics change

Causal inference for decision support

  • A/B test and geo-test analysis
  • Difference-in-differences and event studies
  • Doubly robust and double machine learning workflows
  • Pricing, promotion, and policy-effect estimation from observational data

Why this is different

The current market is crowded with forecasting tools, dashboards, and generic agents. The missing layer is a system that can handle structural instability, exploit modern ML at scale, and still produce business decisions with causal discipline and usable uncertainty.

Break-aware by design

Business time series are not stable forever. We explicitly detect and adapt to regime changes instead of assuming yesterday's structure still holds.

Forecasts with decision value

Point forecasts alone are not enough. We focus on intervals, scenario analysis, and policy relevance so the output supports real operating decisions.

Observational data is first-class

Most important business questions are not randomized. We treat observational causal inference as a core product capability, not an afterthought.

One workflow from model to memo

Outputs become clear decision artifacts: recommended actions, uncertainty summaries, and executive-ready explanations that teams can actually use.

Initial focus: U.S. enterprises with large multi-series business data in pricing, demand planning, revenue forecasting, marketing measurement, and operational policy analysis.

Early use cases

We are initially focused on high-value decisions where prediction alone is not enough and the cost of wrong policy moves is material.

Pricing and promotions

Estimate elasticity, simulate pricing moves, evaluate promotional lift, and distinguish signal from confounding in messy historical data.

Demand and revenue planning

Produce scenario-aware forecasts across many related series, detect shifts quickly, and improve planning under uncertainty.

Marketing and growth measurement

Connect campaign forecasting with causal incrementality analysis so teams can allocate spend with stronger evidence.
