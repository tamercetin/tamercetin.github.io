What “post-axiom” means

The old enterprise stack assumes stable regimes, simple correlations, and reusable formulas for every decision. PostAxiom starts where those assumptions break. We treat forecasting, causal inference, and policy simulation as one connected problem.

Post-forecast-only

Not just point estimates. Probabilistic forecasts, scenario-conditioned forecasts, and decision-value forecasts.

Post-correlation

Not just prediction. Experimental and observational causal estimation for pricing, policy, and operational decisions.

Post-static-regime

Not just one stable world. Break-aware models, linked-series systems, and adaptive learning under structural change.

Illustrative output

A decision memo generated from a pricing and demand scenario for a large multi-series business.

Forecast layer Segment-level demand softened after a regime shift; post-break models now dominate the historical benchmark.
Causal layer Elasticity estimates from observational data indicate room for a controlled price increase with uncertainty still inside decision tolerance.

A different category

Most tools do one of three things: they forecast, they dashboard, or they automate. PostAxiom is being designed to do something harder: estimate what is likely to happen, estimate what would happen if you act, and turn that into a usable decision workflow.

For unstable environments

Business systems shift. Demand regimes break. Policy effects drift. We build around that reality instead of pretending it away.

For high-stakes enterprise choices

Pricing, promotions, budget allocation, supply decisions, growth experiments, and operational policy are the first target domains.

For research-grade trust

Our ambition is not another generic agent. It is a scientific decision layer grounded in econometrics, machine learning, and causal inference.

The platform stack

PostAxiom is being developed as a modular system with three connected engines: forecasting, causality, and decisions.

Forecast Engine

  • Many-series and hierarchical forecasting
  • Global machine learning models for large business datasets
  • Break-aware and regime-aware model selection
  • Probabilistic intervals and scenario-conditioned forecasts
  • Linked-series systems for demand, price, supply, and revenue

Causal Engine

  • Experimental analysis for A/B, geo, and operational tests
  • Observational causal methods for messy enterprise data
  • DML and orthogonal-score workflows
  • Pricing, promotion, and policy-effect estimation
  • Counterfactual simulation under uncertainty

Decision Engine

  • Scenario generation and recommendation memos
  • Action logs and approval workflows
  • Executive summaries grounded in model evidence
  • Monitoring, alerts, and policy updates
  • Human-in-the-loop decision intelligence

Scientific core

The product is meant to look simple on the surface, but the scientific engine behind it is intentionally deep. We want cutting-edge methods where they matter, without turning the platform into a method zoo.

Modern forecasting

State space models, dynamic regression, hierarchical systems, global ML forecasting, nowcasting, and calibrated interval prediction.

Structural instability

Structural break diagnostics, change-point logic, break-aware retraining, and regime-sensitive selection when business dynamics shift.

Linked economic systems

Cointegration-aware and system-level modeling for variables that move together over time instead of being forecast in isolation.

Causal rigor

DML, doubly robust methods, DiD, event studies, synthetic controls, instrumental variables, and experimental analysis where feasible.

Research DNA

PostAxiom is envisioned as a research-native company. The long-run moat is not a prettier interface; it is an intellectual architecture that combines econometrics, machine learning, and enterprise decision systems.

Identification-aware learning

Selection and tuning should not quietly destroy inferential validity. We care about how adaptive pipelines affect credible causal learning.

Leakage-free inference

Cross-fitting, nested model selection, and disciplined data splitting are central when modern ML meets policy-relevant estimation.

Forecasting under instability

Our platform direction assumes structural shifts, time-varying relationships, and linked systems rather than a single permanent data-generating regime.

Founder research agenda The platform direction is aligned with work on double machine learning, observational causal inference, identification-aware model selection, robust inference under weak identification, hierarchical causal models, and decision support under nonstationary environments.

Development path

The company should begin with a narrow commercial wedge, prove decision value, and then expand toward a broader decision intelligence layer.

Phase 1 — Forecast + Pricing Intelligence

Launch with demand, revenue, pricing, and promotion use cases for large multi-series businesses in the U.S. market.

Phase 2 — Policy Lab

Add richer observational causal workflows, controlled experiment analysis, and executive scenario planning for finance and growth teams.

Phase 3 — Decision Layer

Move toward monitored recommendation systems, action logs, adaptive retraining, and human-governed autonomous analytics.
