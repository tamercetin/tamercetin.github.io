Teaching

I teach courses in Econometrics and Data Science for Economists. My teaching emphasizes hands‑on data analysis and real‑world applications to prepare students for research and industry careers with a strong focus on causal inference and machine learning, and the integration of machine learning and causal inference in addition to time series econometrics.

Materials and syllabi for my current and recent courses are listed below.

  • Introductory Applied Econometrics – undergraduate‑level introduction to econometric methods. Course Page
  • Data Science for Economists – exploration of data science techniques for economic applications, with emphasis on machine learning and causal inference. GitHub Repository
